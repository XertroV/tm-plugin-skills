Check these locations for relevant info (adapt to your platform):
- ~/Documents/Trackmania/LogCrash_*.txt
- ~/Documents/Trackmania/LogCrash/*.txt (look for most recent; LogCrash files moved here when game restarts)
- ~/OpenplanetNext/Openplanet.log
- ~/OpenplanetNext/Openplanet-Old.log

Use available tooling to investigate and compe up with hypotheses for the crash, using a binary decision matrix to identify likely candidates.
